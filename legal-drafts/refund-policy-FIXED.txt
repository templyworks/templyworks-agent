<h3>Right of withdrawal for consumers in the EU/EEA</h3>
<p>By law, consumers in the EU/EEA have a 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases. For digital content that is not supplied on a physical medium, this right expires once delivery has begun, if you have expressly consented to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal (&sect; 356(5) BGB / Art. 16(m) EU Consumer Rights Directive). You give this consent with the checkbox before checkout. Full details and the model withdrawal form: <a href="https://templyworks.com/pages/right-of-withdrawal">Right of Withdrawal</a>.</p>
<h3>All sales final after delivery</h3>
<p>Because our templates are instant-access digital goods, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind once the template link has been delivered.</p>
<p><strong>This does not affect your statutory rights.</strong> If your template is faulty or not as described, you are entitled to the remedies provided by law.</p>

<h3>Custom Template Request</h3>
<p>The Custom Template Request is a made-to-order service. EU/EEA consumers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. If you ask us to start work before the withdrawal period ends, your right of withdrawal expires once we have fully delivered the service; if you withdraw after work has started, you pay a proportionate amount for the work done up to that point. See Section 3a of our <a href="https://templyworks.com/policies/terms-of-service">Terms of Service</a>. Once the finished template has been delivered, the order is final; one round of revisions is included.</p>
