## Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG)

### What it means
- Revenue under €22,000/year (2024 threshold) → exempt from Umsatzsteuer (VAT)
- **Do not charge VAT** on any invoice or product listing
- **Do not display a VAT ID** (you don't have one)
- Must include the phrase: *"Gemäß §19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer erhoben."*

## EU Digital Goods Law (Consumer Rights)

### Key rules for digital products sold to EU consumers
- **Right of withdrawal:** 14-day cooling-off period — BUT for digital goods, once download begins, right is waived if customer explicitly consents
- **Implementation:** At checkout, require checkbox: *"Ich stimme zu, dass mit dem Download das Widerrufsrecht erlischt."* (I agree that my right of withdrawal expires upon download)
- **Without this checkbox:** customer can request refund up to 14 days for any reason

### Current refund policy position
TemplyWorks offers goodwill refunds within 30 days (more generous than legally required). This is good for reputation. The legal minimum is: no refunds once download link accessed (if checkbox was ticked at checkout).

## Minors in Business (Germany)

### Kevin's legal situation
- 17 years old → beschränkte Geschäftsfähigkeit (limited legal capacity)
- Commercial activities technically require parental consent (§ 107 BGB)
- For small online businesses under Kleinunternehmerregelung, this is rarely enforced
- **Risk:** In case of a legal dispute, a counterparty could argue Kevin lacks capacity
- **Mitigation:** Get written parental consent document; have a parent co-sign if any major contract is required

### Practical implications
- Shopify account — Shopify's ToS requires 18+. Kevin should have a parent as the account holder or get explicit parental authorization in writing.
- PayPal / payment processing — same situation; verify account is compliant
- Contracts with B2B customers — should involve parent or wait until 18

## Chargeback Protocol

If a chargeback is filed:
1. **Don't panic.** Gather evidence immediately.
2. **Evidence to collect:**
   - Order confirmation email (proof of purchase)
   - EDP delivery email with link (proof of delivery)
   - IP address and timestamp of download (if available from EDP logs)
   - Any customer communication
3. **Submit to Shopify Payments** within the response window (usually 7–21 days)
4. **For amounts under €15** — sometimes more efficient to concede and block the customer
5. **For amounts over €50** — always fight with full evidence
