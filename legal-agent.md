## Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG)

### What it means
- **Do not charge VAT** on any invoice or product listing
- **Do not display a VAT ID**
- Invoices must include: *"Gemäß §19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer erhoben."*

### Thresholds (rules since 1 Jan 2025 — verify with Steuerberater)
- Previous year revenue ≤ **€25,000**, and
- Current year revenue ≤ **€100,000** — exceeding this mid-year ends the exemption **immediately** from that sale onward
- Revenue is in USD at the store → convert to EUR at the booking date for tracking

### EU cross-border digital sales (OSS)
- B2C digital sales to other EU countries: once EU-wide cross-border sales exceed **€10,000/year**, VAT of the customer's country applies (via OSS). Terms of Service already reference this. Monitor it separately from §19.

## EU Digital Goods Law (Consumer Rights)

- 14-day right of withdrawal expires for digital content once delivery starts **if** the customer expressly consented and acknowledged losing the right (§356(5) BGB / Art. 16(m) CRD)
- Terms (Section 3) and Refund Policy cover this; checkout must show the consent step — verify it's actually present at checkout
- Right of Withdrawal page exists at `/pages/right-of-withdrawal`

### Current refund policy (published, July 2026)
- **All sales final once the template link is delivered**
- Broken link / failed delivery / technical access issue reported within 14 days → fixed or refunded
- Accidental duplicate orders refunded if reported within 48 hours
- Statutory rights for defective content unaffected

## Chargeback Protocol

1. Gather evidence immediately:
   - Order confirmation
   - Delivery email with link (proof of delivery)
   - Access timestamp/IP if EDP logs it
   - Withdrawal-waiver consent at checkout
   - Customer communication
2. Submit to Shopify Payments within the response window (usually 7–21 days)
3. Under ~$20 → often more efficient to concede and block the customer
4. Bundles / Custom Requests → fight with full evidence
