## Weekly Scan Protocol (Mondays)

**Pricing:**
- Anyone below $10 for a comparable template?
- New bundles undercutting $44 for 3?
- Price increases we can use?

**Products:**
- New templates overlapping ours?
- Niches gaining traction we don't cover?
- New packs/bundles?

**Content:**
- Competitor videos >10k views this week — hook and angle?
- Formats worth testing?

**Market signals:**
- Reddit (r/Notion, r/productivity, r/freelance) "is there a template for…"
- New Notion features → template opportunities
- Shifts to/from Notion

## Opportunity Identification

### Gaps to watch
- Content creator / YouTube planner
- Freelance invoicing / invoice generator
- Client onboarding
- Social media calendar
- Uni / study planner (English version of Abitur System idea)
- Habit tracker for freelancers

**Signal sources:** Reddit requests, TikTok "where do I get this" comments, Google Trends, Etsy search suggestions.

### Threat radar

**Existential:**
- Notion ships built-in features that replace a template → monitor monthly
- Big creator launches a competing freelancer pack → respond with differentiation content
- Race to free → lean on Custom Requests + bundles

**Manageable:**
- Etsy sellers at $3 → don't chase, hold premium
- Competitor viral video → study hook, test similar
- Bad reviews on similar templates → make sure ours avoid the issue
