## Weekly Scan Protocol

### What to check every Monday

**Pricing changes:**
- Any competitor dropped below €7 per template?
- Any new bundle pricing that undercuts ours?
- Price increases (opportunity to justify our price)?

**New products:**
- Any new template released that overlaps with our 6?
- Any template niche we haven't covered that's gaining traction?
- New "pack" or "bundle" offering from a competitor?

**Content performance:**
- Which competitor TikToks/Reels got >10k views this week?
- What hooks/angles are working for them?
- Any viral format we should replicate?

**Market signals:**
- Trending Notion use cases on Reddit (r/Notion, r/productivity, r/freelance)
- New Notion features that could become template opportunities
- Twitter/X conversations about productivity tools shifting to/from Notion

## Opportunity Identification

### Template gap analysis
Monitor for high-search, low-competition niches:

**Current gaps to watch:**
- "Notion template for content creators" (YouTube/TikTok tracking)
- "Notion habit tracker for freelancers"
- "Notion invoice generator template"
- "Notion client onboarding template"
- "Notion social media calendar template"
- "Notion meeting notes template for freelancers"

**Signal sources:**
- Reddit: r/Notion posts asking "does anyone have a template for..."
- TikTok comments: "where can I get this?" on competitor videos
- Google Trends: rising queries containing "notion template"
- Etsy search suggestions (shows real demand)

### Threat radar

**Existential threats:**
- Notion adds built-in template marketplace that competes directly → monitor notion.so/templates monthly
- Major creator (1M+ followers) launches competing template pack → respond with differentiation content
- Race to free (competitors all go free) → pivot to Custom Request as primary revenue

**Manageable threats:**
- New Etsy seller at €3/template → don't chase, hold premium position
- Competitor TikTok goes viral → study the hook, test similar format
- Bad review about a similar template type → monitor what the complaint is, ensure ours doesn't have same issue
