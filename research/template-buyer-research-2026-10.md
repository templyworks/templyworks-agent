# Template Buyer Research — Oct 2026

Question: if 1,000 people bought each Templyworks template, what helps them keep using it, and what makes them quit?

## Why people abandon Notion templates (all products)
- **Overwhelm on day 1.** Too many databases, fields and pages with no clear starting point. #1 reason setups die.
- **Upkeep > benefit.** Every extra field to fill, tag or file is friction. If maintenance costs more than it saves, they quit within weeks.
- **Pretty but not useful.** Hours on decoration, no daily use. Aesthetic matters for the purchase, function matters for retention.
- **No reminders.** Notion doesn't remind you by default. Deadlines only exist when you open the page.
- **Weak on mobile.** Wide tables and multi-column dashboards are painful on phones.
- **Doesn't fit their workflow.** One rigid "right way" frustrates people.

→ Design rules for every template:
1. One **Focus** area at the top: what matters today / this week. Everything else below or in toggles.
2. Few required fields. Optional fields hidden in default views.
3. Purposeful views per database (focus view first, "All" last).
4. A "set a reminder" tip on every date that matters.
5. Mobile-friendly: list/board views for daily use, tables for setup.
6. Same visual system everywhere: green header callout, emoji H2 sections, gray helper text, guides in toggles.

## Per template

### Finance HQ
- Best finance templates all cover: transactions, budgets, goals/sinking funds, recurring bills, charts. ✅ we have.
- Common in top sellers, missing in ours: **accounts & net worth**, **debts**, **upcoming bills this month**.
- Freelancers: need a **tax reserve**.
- Fix: add Accounts & Net Worth DB, "Renewing soon" subscription view, tax-reserve goal, green charts (currently purple = off-brand).

### Client Pipeline (CRM)
- Freelancers fail at follow-up, not at storing contacts. Key view: **follow-ups due today/this week**.
- Flat lists = no value; linked Contacts ↔ Deals ↔ Interactions is the value. ✅ we have.
- Fix: follow-up focus views, deal board by stage, "where leads come from" chart.

### Project Tracker
- 67% of freelancers absorb scope creep on every project; unpaid extra work is the biggest money leak.
- Most-used view: **everything due in the next 7 days across all clients.**
- Fix: add Scope Changes log (extra hours/fees, client approved), "Due next 7 days" view, board by phase.

### Pitch Kit
- Win-rate drivers: reply < 24h (~2× win rate), follow-up within 48h (+30%), case studies (+52% perceived value), clear pricing table early.
- ✅ Already added: case studies, follow-up scripts, example proposal.
- Fix: win-rate chart, follow-up focus view.

### Second Brain
- Second brains die from **filing friction at capture** and **skipped weekly reviews**.
- Fix: capture = title only, filing later. Inbox "To process" view, Resources / Archive views, Ideas board. Weekly review front and center. ✅ Projects/Areas/Weekly Review added today.

### Client Portal
- Clients need: project status, next deadlines, deliverables, feedback place, invoice status. View-only for clients (clients editing = chaos).
- Fix: client-facing "Project at a glance" + "Next up" section at top, invoice statuses Draft/Sent/Paid/Overdue, freelancer setup note on view-only sharing.

### Student OS
- Students value: easy start, daily dashboard, assignments + exams, weighted grades, mobile.
- Pain: no reminders, last-minute cramming (no spaced revision), group project chaos.
- Fix: Study Sessions DB (minutes per course, method), focus views (due this week, calendar, board), spaced-revision plan in exams.
